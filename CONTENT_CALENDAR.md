## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Every Guide

1. **Search Intent Verification:** Confirm searchers seek actionable instructions rather than abstract definitions.
2. **Tool Integration:** Every article must link to its counterpart ToolBox utility within the first 200 words and include an embedded CTA block.
3. **Information Gain:** Include original diagrams, verified formulas, or code blocks that competitors omit.
4. **Structured Data:** Emit valid `Article` JSON-LD schema with author, publisher, datePublished, dateModified, and headline.
5. **No Cannibalization:** Ensure the article targets informational intent ("how to ...", "guide to ...") and links to the transactional tool ("... calculator", "... converter").
